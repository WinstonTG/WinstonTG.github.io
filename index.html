Thesis

AI should be a systems layer—not another tab.

I’m interested in what comes after the chatbot era: persistent agents that can maintain context, use trusted tools, operate software, and work across long-running tasks without losing the thread.

My work sits between systems engineering, research, and product design. I care about the infrastructure around intelligence—memory, authority, observability, execution, and the interfaces that let people stay in control.
